I’m Lokesh, a product designer in Bengaluru.

B.Tech ’22, IIT Guwahati

I studied chemical engineering at IIT Guwahati. Somewhere in the middle of that I became the campus photographer, which is how this started — a camera, then Photoshop, then Adobe XD, and no idea that what I was doing already had a name.

I found out it did, and that people were paid to do it. No degree, no bootcamp — I taught myself, graduating in December 2022 into a market with no appetite for juniors. It took four months of making work nobody asked for before someone paid me for it.

Since then I’ve mostly been the only designer on the product, which taught me the parts of the job nobody assigns you and what solo work costs. I learned to pressure-test my own thinking because there was nobody else to do it. That story is in the case studies.

I’m a stubborn believer that if you can’t explain a number, you shouldn’t put it on a slide.

When I log off…

Gym, sport, and travel when I can.

Beyond design

I taught a session on personal finance

I’m interested in personal finance and investing, and HR asked whether I’d run a session for the team. So I did — personal finance and derivatives, one afternoon in June 2024.

It’s the same job as design: take something people avoid because it looks complicated, and make the first step obvious.
