About

I learned to look before I learned to design.

No design degree, no bootcamp. I taught myself.

IIT Guwahati

Somewhere in the middle of my degree I became the campus photographer. That’s where the looking started.

Self-taught

A camera, then Photoshop, then Adobe XD, and no idea that what I was doing already had a name. I found out it did, and that people were paid to do it. No degree, no bootcamp.

December 2022

I graduated into a market with no appetite for juniors. It took four months of making work nobody asked for before someone paid me for it.

Since then

Mostly the only designer on the product, which taught me the parts of the job nobody assigns you and what solo work costs. I learned to pressure-test my own thinking because there was nobody else to do it. That story is in the case studies.

Beyond design

I taught a session on personal finance

I’m interested in personal finance and investing, and HR asked whether I’d run a session for the team. So I did. Personal finance and derivatives, one afternoon in June 2024.

It’s the same job as design: take something people avoid because it looks complicated, and make the first step obvious.
