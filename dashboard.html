Method, and what this cannot tell me

The first read was mostly me. 381 views from 17 visitors — 22 pages each. Local files were being tracked, one page was counted four ways (/, /index.html, /#top, /index.html#top), and event properties never reached the reports. I fixed collection and reset the dataset rather than clean it.

Small numbers do not get percentages. Any rate on fewer than 20 sessions shows as a raw count and is flagged. Two to four is not +100%, it is two more people.

Encoding. Position on a common scale for every comparison, length where position will not do. The retention charts share one vertical axis; the source chart is a lollipop on a single axis. No donuts, no gauges, no dual axes.

What it cannot tell me. Reading depth is a proxy for attention, not interest. An untagged direct visit is unattributable. A company link opening does not prove a person opened it — some applicant tracking systems fetch every link in a submission. At this volume a two-visit difference is not a trend.
