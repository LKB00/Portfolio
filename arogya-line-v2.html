Her day starts with who needs her

The ASHA’s phone is a work queue, not a dashboard. It starts from the families she already knows, ranks people by what is due, and puts the next action in words.

How to try Arogya Line

The prototype runs in any browser. A guided walkthrough takes you through one patient: an offline booking, the doctor’s consult, and the advice heard on the line. It is a concept, not a production healthcare system, and every name and number is invented.
