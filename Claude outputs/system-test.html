04

Home breaks the pattern, on purpose.

Every other screen carries a product bar. Home does not, and that is the one place the merge has to prove itself.

The call

Whether Home follows the same product-tab pattern as every other screen.

Considered

Give Home the same product bar. Consistent with Explore, Orders and Portfolio, and you always know which product you are in.

Chose

No tabs on Home. Every product visible at once. With tabs, a trader on Home only ever sees stocks, and the merge exists so they see funds too.

Cost

One screen breaks the pattern, and Home becomes the only place where scope is not explicit.
